How the shortlist works

  1. 01Same position

    Defenders with defenders, midfielders with midfielders, and forwards with forwards. Goalkeepers are excluded.

  2. 02Within budget

    A candidate can cost at most the share of the reference player’s value you set.

  3. 03Similarity score

    Twelve per-90 metrics covering shooting, passing, progression, and ball recovery. The model compares profile shape, not raw volume.

  4. 04Out-of-time validation

    The 2024/25 season was not used to build the shortlist. It is shown only to check what happened next.
